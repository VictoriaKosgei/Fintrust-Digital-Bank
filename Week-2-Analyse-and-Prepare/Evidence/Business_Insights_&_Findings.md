# FinTrust Business Findings & Insights

This document outlines the key business findings and insights derived from the performance data analysis of the FinTrust banking platform.

---

### 📊 Finding 1 — FinTrust has strong transaction activity
* **Finding:** FinTrust customers are actively using the banking platform across the analysed period.
* **Evidence:** The dataset contains **1,500 customers** and **12,000 transactions**, with a total transaction value of approximately **NGN 560.48 million**.
* **Business Meaning:** The transaction volume and value provide a useful baseline for understanding customer activity and overall transaction performance. FinTrust can use these measures to monitor changes in customer activity over time.

---

### 📱 Finding 2 — Mobile App is the most-used transaction channel
* **Finding:** Customers rely heavily on the Mobile App for their transactions.
* **Evidence:** The *Transaction Channels* visual shows the **Mobile App** as the channel with the highest transaction volume compared with POS, Web, ATM, and USSD.
* **Business Meaning:** Mobile banking is an important part of the customer experience. FinTrust should therefore pay particular attention to the reliability and usability of the Mobile App because disruption to this channel could affect a large portion of transaction activity.

---

### 💳 Finding 3 — Transfers and Card Purchases drive transaction activity
* **Finding:** Transfers and Card Purchases are the two largest transaction types by transaction volume.
* **Evidence:** The *Transaction Types* visual shows **Transfer** and **Card Purchase** ahead of Bill Payment, Cash Withdrawal, Deposit, and Airtime/Data.
* **Business Meaning:** These transaction types represent major areas of customer activity. FinTrust can use this information when prioritising service reliability, transaction monitoring, and improvements to the customer transaction experience.

---

### ✅ Finding 4 — Most transactions are successful
* **Finding:** The large majority of transactions reach a successful outcome.
* **Evidence:** The dashboard reports a **90.47% Transaction Success Rate**.
* **Business Meaning:** Most customer transactions are completed successfully, which provides an indication of generally successful transaction processing. However, the remaining failed, pending, and reversed transactions should be monitored to identify operational problems and opportunities to improve transaction reliability.

---

### ⚠️ Finding 5 — Risk-review activity requires further investigation
* **Finding:** A meaningful proportion of FinTrust transactions are flagged for risk review.
* **Evidence:** The dashboard reports a **19.60% Risk Review Rate**.
* **Business Meaning:** Risk-review activity is significant enough to warrant further analysis. FinTrust could investigate whether flagged transactions are associated with particular transaction types, channels, customer segments, transaction values, or international transactions. This could help the bank better understand the characteristics of transactions entering the review process.

---

### 📈 Finding 6 — Transaction activity changes over time
* **Finding:** Transaction activity varies across the analysed months rather than remaining constant.
* **Evidence:** The *Transaction Trend* visual shows changes in transaction volume across January, February, and March, with activity reaching its **highest point in March**.
* **Business Meaning:** Changes in transaction activity over time can help FinTrust identify periods of increased or reduced customer activity. Monitoring these trends can support operational planning and help the bank investigate factors contributing to changes in transaction volume.
