# Week 3 — FinTrust Advanced Business Intelligence

## Project Overview

Week 3 of the AnalystLab Africa Data Analytics Internship Program focused on developing the existing FinTrust Financial Intelligence & Digital Banking Support Solution into a more complete, analytical, and decision-oriented business intelligence solution.

Building directly on the Week 1 business and data assessment and the Week 2 data preparation and exploratory analysis, this stage extended SQL and Python analysis, enhanced the Power BI dashboard, validated major business findings, and developed evidence-based management recommendations.

**Project:** FinTrust Financial Intelligence & Digital Banking Support Solution
**Program:** AnalystLab Africa Data Analytics Internship Program
**Track:** Data Analytics
**Week:** 3 — Advanced Business Intelligence & Dashboard Development

## Objectives

* Review and improve the existing Week 2 analysis.
* Conduct deeper SQL business analysis.
* Extend Python exploratory and comparative analysis.
* Develop the existing Power BI dashboard with advanced analytical visuals.
* Validate major business findings against supporting evidence.
* Analyse key performance indicators and transaction patterns.
* Develop evidence-based management recommendations.
* Document testing and validation activities in preparation for Week 4.

## Part A — Review of Previous Work

Reviewed the Week 1 business and data assessment and the Week 2 Excel, SQL, Python, and Power BI deliverables.

The review identified the need for deeper SQL analysis, additional comparative Python analysis, improved dashboard interactivity, validation of major findings, and evidence-based management recommendations.

## Part B — Advanced SQL Analysis

Extended the Week 2 SQL work with eight deeper business analyses covering:

1. Customer transaction behaviour by segment.
2. Transaction value and performance by channel.
3. Successful and failed transactions by channel and transaction type.
4. Risk-review patterns.
5. International versus domestic transaction activity.
6. Customer-level transaction frequency and activity segmentation.
7. High-value transaction patterns.
8. Monthly transaction volume, value, and success-rate trends.

The analysis used techniques including joins, aggregations, conditional calculations, CTEs, subqueries, and window functions where appropriate.

**Deliverable:** `FinTrust_Week3_Advanced_SQL_Analysis.sql`

## Part C — Advanced Python Analysis

Extended the Week 2 exploratory analysis with six additional analytical outputs:

1. Customer segment comparison.
2. Channel performance analysis.
3. International versus domestic analysis.
4. Customer activity segmentation.
5. Risk-review analysis by transaction characteristics.
6. Monthly transaction trend analysis.

These analyses were designed to provide deeper comparisons, customer segmentation, trend analysis, and additional evidence for validating earlier findings.

**Deliverable:** `FinTrust_Week3_Advanced_Python_Analysis.ipynb`

## Part D — Power BI Dashboard Development

Developed a new **Advanced Business Insights** page while preserving the original Week 2 overview page.

The Week 3 page extends the original analysis through:

* Average transactions per customer by segment.
* Transaction success rate by channel.
* International versus domestic transaction value and volume.
* Risk-review flags by transaction type.
* Monthly transaction success-rate trends.
* Transaction value by channel.
* KPI cards and interactive slicers.

The dashboard also includes a key business insights panel. The report is designed to support comparisons and deeper investigation rather than simply reproduce the Week 2 overview.

**Deliverable:** `FinTrust_Week3_Advanced_BI_Dashboard.pbix`

## Part E — Validated Business Findings

Selected and reviewed major findings covering:

* Overall transaction performance.
* Differences in channel success rates.
* Domestic versus international transaction value.
* Risk-review share of transaction value.
* Risk-review patterns by transaction type.
* Monthly transaction success-rate trends.

The findings use the required structure:

**Finding → Evidence → Validation → Business Meaning**

The validation outcome is documented for each finding, identifying whether the evidence supports the original conclusion or whether revision is necessary.

**Deliverable:** `Week3_Validated_Business_Findings.md`

## Part F — Management Recommendations

Developed six recommendations based on the analysis:

1. Investigate Mobile App transaction performance.
2. Review high-value risk-flagged transactions.
3. Examine Transfer and Cash Withdrawal risk-review patterns.
4. Monitor monthly transaction success rates.
5. Use domestic and international analysis to support business planning.
6. Investigate the causes of unsuccessful transactions.

Each recommendation follows:

**Finding → Evidence → Business Implication → Recommended Action**

Recommendations focus on practical investigation and monitoring, without treating risk-review flags as proof of fraud.

**Deliverable:** `Week3_Management_Recommendations.md`
