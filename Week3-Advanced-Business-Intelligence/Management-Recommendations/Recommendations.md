# Management Recommendations

Based on the Week 3 SQL analysis and validated business findings, the following recommendations are proposed for FinTrust management.

### 1. Improve Mobile App Transaction Reliability

The Mobile App recorded the lowest transaction success rate at **89.75%**, compared with **91.70% for ATM** and above 90% for the other channels.

**Management Recommendation:**
Management should prioritize a deeper review of Mobile App transaction failures and identify the transaction types, statuses, and periods contributing most to unsuccessful transactions. Improvements should be targeted at the specific failure patterns identified through further analysis.

---

### 2. Strengthen Risk Monitoring for Transfers and Cash Withdrawals

**Transfer (28.49%)** and **Cash Withdrawal (25.31%)** recorded the highest risk-review shares among the analysed transaction types.

**Management Recommendation:**
Management should strengthen monitoring of Transfer and Cash Withdrawal activity and conduct further analysis of their transaction amounts, channels, outcomes, and domestic/international classification to identify recurring risk patterns.

---

### 3. Prioritize High-Value Risk-Reviewed Transactions

Risk-reviewed transactions represented **19.60% of transaction count** but approximately **28.87% of total transaction value**, with about **NGN 161.78M** associated with risk-reviewed activity.

**Management Recommendation:**
Management should prioritize the review of high-value transactions carrying risk-review flags and use transaction characteristics to support more focused risk-monitoring and review processes.

---

### 4. Establish Continuous Transaction Performance Monitoring

Transaction success remained above **90%** across January, February, and March, but performance varied slightly between months.

**Management Recommendation:**
Management should implement continuous monitoring of transaction success rates using KPIs segmented by **month, channel, and transaction type**. This would help identify meaningful changes in transaction performance early and support timely investigation.

---

### 5. Maintain Focus on Domestic Transaction Services

Domestic transactions generated approximately **NGN 0.54B**, compared with approximately **NGN 0.02B** from international transactions.

**Management Recommendation:**
Management should continue prioritizing the reliability and performance of domestic transaction services, while maintaining separate monitoring of international transactions to understand their transaction value and risk characteristics.

---
