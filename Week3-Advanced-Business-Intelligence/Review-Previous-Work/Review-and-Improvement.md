# Week 3 — Part A: Review of Previous Work

## 1. Purpose

Before beginning Week 3, the Week 1 and Week 2 FinTrust work was reviewed to identify completed outputs, areas requiring further development, and opportunities for deeper analysis.

Week 3 builds directly on the existing FinTrust project rather than starting a new project or replacing the existing datasets.

---

## 2. Review of Previous Work

### Week 1

Week 1 focused on understanding and planning the FinTrust business and data environment.

Completed activities included:

- Business understanding
- Stakeholder identification
- Customer and transaction data profiling
- Data quality assessment
- Customer–transaction relationship assessment
- Analytical question development
- KPI planning
- Dashboard wireframe development
- Planning for Weeks 2–4

### Week 2

Week 2 moved from planning into practical analysis.

Completed activities included:

- Data preparation and quality assessment using Excel
- Eight business-driven SQL analyses
- Exploratory Data Analysis using Python
- Data visualizations
- Customer, transaction, channel, status, international and risk-review analysis
- Power BI management dashboard development
- KPI analysis
- Business findings using the structure:
  **Finding → Evidence → Business Meaning**

---

## 3. Areas Requiring Improvement

| Issue / Gap | Evidence | Required Improvement |
|---|---|---|
| SQL analysis was mainly foundational | Week 2 included eight business questions covering major transaction and customer areas. | Develop additional advanced SQL analyses covering customer-level behaviour, channels, risk, international activity, high-value transactions and trends. |
| Python analysis requires deeper investigation | Week 2 explored the main customer and transaction dimensions. | Add deeper comparative, trend, customer-level and segmentation analysis with additional visualizations. |
| Power BI was an initial dashboard | Week 2 dashboard included KPIs, trends, customer segments, transaction types, channels, status and risk-review analysis. | Improve analytical depth, interactivity, KPI analysis, visual consistency and decision-oriented storytelling. |
| Business findings require validation | Week 2 findings followed Finding → Evidence → Business Meaning. | Validate at least five major findings using deeper analysis and determine whether each finding remains supported or requires revision. |
| Management recommendations were not yet a full deliverable | Week 2 focused on findings and business meaning. | Develop evidence-based recommendations using Finding → Evidence → Business Implication → Recommended Action. |

---

## 4. Week 3 Improvement Direction

The Week 3 development will follow:

**Week 2 Output → Improvement / Development → Validation → Improved Output**

The main areas of development will be:

1. Advanced SQL business analysis
2. Deeper Python exploratory analysis
3. Improved Power BI dashboard
4. Validation of major business findings
5. Evidence-based management recommendations
6. Testing and validation evidence

---

## 5. Expected Week 3 Outcome

By the end of Week 3, the FinTrust solution should provide a more complete and decision-oriented view of customer and transaction activity.

The improved solution will be prepared for the Week 4 stages of:

**Final Testing → Refinement → Documentation → Presentation**
