# Week 3 — Review Previous Work

## 1. Purpose

Before beginning Week 3, the previous FinTrust work from Week 1 and Week 2 was reviewed.

The purpose of this review was to identify completed work, areas requiring improvement or deeper analysis, and the development required to create a more complete business intelligence solution.

Week 3 continues directly from the existing FinTrust project and does not introduce a new project or unrelated dataset.

---

## 2. Week 1 Review

Week 1 focused on understanding the FinTrust business and data environment and writing a report.

### Completed Work

- Business understanding
- Management questions
- Stakeholder identification
- Customer and transaction data profiling
- Data quality assessment
- Customer–transaction relationship assessment
- Analytical questions
- KPI planning
- Dashboard wireframe
- Week 2–4 project planning

### Week 1 Outcome

Week 1 established the business and data foundation required for detailed analysis.

## 4. Areas Requiring Improvement

| Issue / Gap | Evidence | Required Improvement |
|---|---|---|
| SQL analysis needs greater depth | Week 2 completed eight foundational business analyses. | Develop additional and deeper SQL analyses covering customer behaviour, transaction value, channels, risk, international activity, high-value transactions and trends. |
| Python analysis needs deeper comparative analysis | Week 2 explored the main customer and transaction dimensions. | Extend the analysis with comparative, trend, customer-level and segmentation analysis and additional visualizations. |
| Power BI needs further development | Week 2 produced an initial management dashboard. | Improve analytical depth, interactivity, visual organization, KPI analysis and decision-oriented storytelling. |
| Business findings require validation | Week 2 findings were based on the initial analysis. | Select at least five major findings and validate them using deeper analysis. |
| Management recommendations need to be developed | Week 2 focused on findings and business meaning. | Convert validated findings into evidence-based management recommendations. |
