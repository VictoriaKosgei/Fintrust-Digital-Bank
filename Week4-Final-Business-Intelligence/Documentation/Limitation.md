# Project Limitations

1. The dataset is an educational project dataset and may not represent all real-world banking activity.
2. Risk-review flags do not confirm fraud, suspicious intent, or financial loss.
3. The analysis identifies differences and patterns but does not independently establish their causes.
4. The monthly transaction success-rate comparison covers three months, which is insufficient to establish a long-term trend.
5. The available data may not contain all operational details needed to explain failed transactions or channel performance differences.
6. Recommendations require additional investigation and operational evidence before implementation.
7. Dashboard testing conclusions must be based on the actual behavior observed during the final test.
