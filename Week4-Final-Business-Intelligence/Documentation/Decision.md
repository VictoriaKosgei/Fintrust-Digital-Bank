# Project Decisions

* The project was organized into four weekly stages to maintain a clear record of its progression.
* Customer and transaction datasets were related through `Customer_ID`.
* SQL was used for structured business queries and aggregation.
* Python was used for exploratory data analysis and visualization.
* Power BI was used to present KPIs, trends, customer and transaction analysis, and risk-review patterns.
* The Week 3 Power BI dashboard was selected as the starting point for the Week 4 final dashboard to preserve earlier work and avoid unnecessary rebuilding.
* The final dashboard is saved as a separate Week 4 version.
* The monthly SQL trend query was corrected to parse the text-formatted transaction date-time field.
* Large video evidence is linked externally when direct GitHub upload exceeds the browser limit.
* Business recommendations were based on recorded analytical results, with limitations stated where causes or outcomes could not be established.
