# FinTrust Digital Bank — Power BI Dashboard Testing

## 1. Objective

The purpose of this testing process was to verify the accuracy, functionality, clarity, and usability of the final FinTrust Digital Bank Power BI dashboard.

The testing was conducted as part of Week 4 of the **AnalystLab Africa Data Analytics Internship** to ensure that the dashboard was ready for final project reporting and presentation.

## 2. Dashboard Overview

The Power BI dashboard presents key information about FinTrust's transaction activity, transaction performance, channel performance, domestic and international transactions, and risk-review patterns.

The final dashboard was reviewed to assess the reliability of its displayed metrics, the accuracy of its visualisations, the functionality of its interactive features, and its overall presentation.

## 4. Testing Observations

The final review covered the dashboard's analytical and interactive components.

### 4.1 KPI Calculations

The dashboard's key performance indicators were reviewed against the corresponding analysis results. This check was intended to identify inconsistencies between the reported metrics and the underlying calculations.

### 4.2 Monthly Transaction Trends

Monthly transaction trends and success rates were reviewed to ensure that the visualisations represented the transaction data and time-based calculations appropriately.

### 4.3 Channel Performance

The channel performance visuals were examined to assess how transaction outcomes differed across the available channels.

### 4.4 Domestic and International Transactions

The domestic and international transaction comparisons were reviewed to ensure that the relevant categories and displayed values were represented appropriately.

### 4.5 Risk-Review Analysis

Risk-review metrics were examined to understand the volume and value of transactions flagged for further review. These flags were treated as indicators for investigation, not proof of fraudulent activity.

### 4.6 Slicer Functionality

Slicers were tested to examine whether selecting filters updated the relevant visuals as intended.

### 4.7 Chart Interactions

Chart interactions were tested to check whether supported selections, filtering, and highlighting behaved as expected.

### 4.8 Visual Clarity and Usability

The final layout, KPI cards, titles, labels, and charts were reviewed for consistency, readability, and ease of interpretation.

## 7. Validation Considerations

Dashboard testing was complemented by the project's SQL, Python, and data-quality analysis. These checks help establish whether the dashboard's reported values are consistent with the underlying dataset and agreed KPI definitions.

Visual inspection alone does not prove that every calculation is correct. Any unresolved discrepancies should be documented before the final results are presented.

The findings apply to the supplied educational project dataset and should not automatically be generalised to a live banking environment.
