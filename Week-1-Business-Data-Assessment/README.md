# FinTrust Financial Intelligence & Digital Banking Support Solution

## Week 1 — Business & Data Intelligence Assessment

### Project Overview

This project is part of the FinTrust Financial Intelligence & Digital Banking Support Solution. The Week 1 Data Analytics task focused on understanding the business problem, exploring the available data, defining analytical objectives, identifying key questions and KPIs, and planning the next stages of the project.

### Track

**Data Analytics**

### Week 1 Focus

* Understand the business problem and project context
* Explore the available customer and transaction datasets
* Profile the datasets and assess data quality
* Define analytical questions and KPIs
* Identify assumptions, risks, and limitations
* Design an initial dashboard wireframe
* Plan the activities for Weeks 2–4

### Datasets

The project uses two main datasets:

* **Customer Data** — contains customer demographic and profile information.
* **Transaction Data** — contains information about banking transactions and related attributes.

The datasets are connected using `Customer_ID`, allowing customer-level information to be analysed alongside transaction activity.

### Key Areas for Analysis

The planned analysis will investigate:

* Customer demographics and segments
* Transaction volume and transaction value
* Transaction types
* Banking channels
* Transaction outcomes
* Digital engagement
* International transactions
* Risk Review Flags
* Customer and transaction patterns over time

### Data Quality Assessment

Initial profiling was performed on both datasets to understand their structure and identify potential data-quality issues.

The assessment included:

* Number of records and columns
* Missing values
* Duplicate records
* Unique identifiers
* Data types
* Customer–transaction relationships
* Potential inconsistencies requiring further investigation

The identified issues will be investigated and addressed during the data preparation stage.

### Dashboard Planning

A dashboard wireframe was created during Week 1 to plan the structure of the future Power BI dashboard.

The dashboard will be developed during the later project stages after the data has been cleaned, validated, and analysed.

### Week 2–4 Plan

**Week 2 — Data Preparation & Exploratory Analysis**

* Clean and prepare the datasets
* Investigate missing and inconsistent values
* Validate customer–transaction relationships
* Perform exploratory analysis
* Calculate initial analytical measures

**Week 3 — Dashboard Development & Deeper Analysis**

* Develop the Power BI dashboard
* Create and validate KPIs
* Analyse transaction and customer patterns
* Investigate channels, outcomes, international activity, and risk indicators
* Refine the analytical findings

**Week 4 — Validation & Finalization**

* Validate the dashboard and findings
* Finalize KPIs and visualizations
* Develop evidence-based insights and recommendations
* Document assumptions and limitations
* Prepare the final project outputs
